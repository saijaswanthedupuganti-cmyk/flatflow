# Oddroof words (one name for each thing)

Use exactly these words in every screen, button, title, toast and empty state. Sentence case everywhere
("Add task", never "Add Task"). Data/backend values stay as they are; only what people read changes.

## What Oddroof is (say it on Welcome, Sign up and the first-choice screen)
**Oddroof keeps your flat in order. Split expenses, share chores and bills, and find a flat or a flatmate.**

## Feedback after any save
- Short message with what happened: "Expense added", "Task added", "Bill saved", "Payment recorded".
- Offer Undo where the action can be undone.
- Never close a form without saying whether it saved.
- Leaving a form with typed input asks: "Discard changes?" · Keep editing / Discard.
- Buttons are never disabled without a reason: keep them enabled and show the field error on tap.
