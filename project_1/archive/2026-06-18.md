## 1. What Is Habitiq?

Habitiq is a **shared living management platform** that automates duty rotation among flatmates. It gives every flat a transparent, real-time system for chore management — eliminating arguments, forgotten tasks, and the awkward "whose turn is it?" conversation.

**The name:**
- **Habitat** — the shared space where people live together
- **Habit** — the recurring routines of shared living that repeat and rotate
- **IQ** — the intelligence that skips absent members, reassigns overdues, tracks reliability

**Live at:** https://habitiq.app

---

## 2. The Problem

Shared living breaks down in predictable ways:

| The Situation | What Actually Happens |
|---|---|
| Verbal chore assignments | Forgotten by Tuesday |
| WhatsApp reminders | Ignored, or causes tension |
| "Your turn" arguments | Nobody agrees on the count |
| Friend going out suddenly | Task skipped, no coverage |
| Group goes out together | Everything piles up, no accountability |
| New person joins the flat | Nobody updates the roster |

**Nobody wants to be the flat manager.** But without a system, someone always ends up being one — resentfully.

---

## 3. Who It's For

### Primary Audience
Friend groups sharing a flat — 2 to 8 people, age 18–30, living together in rented accommodation in Indian metro cities (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi).

### Situations Where Habitiq Is Essential

**The Sudden Absence:** One person gets caught in the rain, travels last minute, or goes out of station for a few days. Without Habitiq, their tasks pile up. With it — the system automatically skips them and reassigns, then puts them back when they return.

**The Group Outing:** Everyone goes out together for a weekend. Tasks due that day get marked, coverage gets requested, no confusion about who owes what when everyone returns.

**The New Joiner:** A friend moves in mid-month. Without a system, nobody knows when they "start" on duties. Habitiq adds them to every rotation queue instantly.

**The Passive Flatmate:** One person never initiates but will follow a clear system. Habitiq makes the system the authority — so no one has to nag.

**The Organised Flatmate:** The person who tracks everything in their head, frustrated that others don't. Habitiq removes their burden and makes fairness visible to everyone.

## 5. Features — What Exists Today (v0.1.0)

### Authentication & Onboarding
- Google Sign-In (one tap, works on mobile + desktop)
- Email/password login
- Custom auth domain proxy (fixes iOS Safari authentication bug)
- Create a flat → get unique invite code instantly
- Join a flat via invite code
- Flat live and running in under 2 minutes

### Smart Rotation Engine (The Core)
The algorithm that makes Habitiq work — not a simple round-robin.

- Auto-assigns each task to the next person in queue when it comes due
- Skips members marked Out of Station (OOS), resumes them at correct position when they return
- Overdue tasks persist and carry over — nothing gets quietly dropped
- Admin can manually override assignment at any time
- Task frequencies: daily / weekly / fortnightly / monthly
- Priority levels: low / medium / high
- Custom start date per task

### Task Management
- Admin creates, edits, and deletes tasks
- Custom emoji per task
- One-tap mark complete
- Task completion date editing (retroactive)
- Overdue tracking with visual indicators
- New members auto-added to all rotation queues immediately

### Swap Request System
- Any member can request coverage from any other member
- Formal accept / decline flow (not a chat message)
- Persistent banner on dashboard until resolved
- Side-by-side Received / Sent view (newest first)
- Full history log

### Rotation Order Card
- Every member sees the full queue for every task
- Position badges including "YOU" marker
- Due date display, frequency badge
- Projected turn dates per person

### Admin Controls
- My Tasks view (admin's personal assignments)

### Membership Management
- Member can voluntarily leave a flat

- All task queues update automatically on leave/kick
- Kicked users see a clean "you've been removed" message

- One account, multiple flats

## 11. Member Home Screen — Design (This Session)

### What This Screen Is

The member home screen is the primary interface for a non-admin flatmate. Its job: tell a member, at a glance, everything they need to know about their home in one scroll.

This is the "OS for a shared home" — not an expense tracker with chores bolted on, not a task manager with a money tab added. Every decision is guided by one question: **what does someone actually need when they open this app?**

### Design Philosophy

**Home first.** The first thing a member sees is their home — flat name, flatmates, who's home, who's out. Before tasks, before money. The emotional anchor is "this is my home."

**Action first.** The most important interaction is completing a task. The task name is the largest element on the page — 40px, weight 900. Nothing competes with it.

**Statistics second.** Reliability scores and task counts exist to inform — not dominate. They sit below the fold, compact and scannable.

**Personality third.** Time-based greetings, presence indicators, and warm dark colors give the screen personality. This is a home app, not a productivity dashboard.

### Section Hierarchy (7 sections in scroll order)

**1. Home Identity**
- Flat name and date
- Time-of-day greeting: "Good morning, Sai."
- Contextual sub-text changes by time of day
- Member avatar stack: all flatmates overlapping
- Live presence indicator: green dot + "Bhanu home"
- Time slots: morning (5am–12pm), afternoon (12–5pm), evening (5–9pm), night (9pm–5am)

**2. Today's Tasks** — the visual hero
- Section label: "Your tasks" + live progress: "1 of 3 done ▓░░"
- Violet top border accent (2px) marks this as primary section
- Primary task at 40px/900 — biggest element on page
- Status badge: "Due today" (violet) or "Done ✓" (green)
- Rotation queue: YOUR avatar (full) → next members (faded, with names)
- CTAs: [Mark as Done] (violet primary) + [Swap] (secondary)
- Additional tasks as tappable rows with: completion circle, task name, due date, chevron
- Holds 3–4 tasks cleanly

**Completed task state:**
- Strikethrough + muted color on task name
- Badge switches to green "Done ✓"
- CTA replaced by: [✓ Completed today] row (green background)
- Subtle green tint on the whole section `rgba(86,179,116,0.04)`
- Progress bar advances

**3. Home Health**
- Three inline stats: Reliability score (with 3px progress bar) + Open tasks count + Days to next review
- "#1 this week" shown in green if applicable

**4. Bills — Conditional Section**
- Appears ONLY when a bill is within ~7 days of due date
- Never mixed with expense splits
- Shows: bill name, due date, amount, split method, [Pay] button
- Red "1 pending" urgency badge
- When no bills are near due: section disappears entirely

**5. Money — Expense Splits Only**
- Never contains recurring bills
- Shows: total owed to you, from whom, with label "from 2 people · expense splits"
- Individual rows: avatar + name + context + green amount
- [Settle up →] link

**6. Activity Feed**
- What happened in the flat recently
- Each item: avatar + sentence + timestamp
- Events: expense additions, task completions, swap acceptances, bill splits
- [See all] → full activity log

**7. Upcoming Rotations**
- Horizontal scrollable carousel
- Each slot: date label + avatar + name
- YOUR turns: violet label, violet avatar border, "YOU" label
- Others: muted gray
- No task names — just who + when (clean, scannable)

### Bills vs Money — The Separation Rule

**Bills** = recurring obligations on a cycle (electricity, internet, rent, subscriptions). They have a due date. They need to be paid. They are temporary — you pay them and they go away until next cycle.

**Money / Expense Splits** = one-off shared costs that need to be tracked and settled (groceries, a dinner, cleaning supplies). They create a balance that needs reconciliation.

These two financial concepts must always be displayed separately because:
1. They have different urgency (a due bill needs action today; a split can wait)
2. They have different resolution (pay a bill; settle up with a person)
3. Mixing them creates a confusing, finance-app-like experience that contradicts the home-first philosophy

**Rule:** Bills section appears only when at least one bill is due within ~7 days. When no bills are approaching, the Bills section is hidden entirely. The Money section always shows only expense splits, never bill items.

### Task Detail — Bottom Sheet

Tapping any task opens a bottom sheet sliding up from screen edge — not a new page, not a dialog.

**Sheet content:**
- Drag handle (dismissable by swipe or backdrop tap)
- Task name (34px, 900 weight)
- Status badge
- Frequency + assignment info
- Full rotation queue: all member avatars with names, YOU highlighted in violet
- Historical note: who last completed this and when
- CTAs: [Mark as Done] + [Swap] if pending; [Completed today ✓] if done

**Animation:** slides up with spring physics — `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`, 320ms. Semi-transparent backdrop closes on tap.

## 12. Navigation Redesign (This Session)

Old navigation: **Dashboard · Expenses · + · Chores · More**

New navigation: **Home · Duties · Log · Money · Nest**

| Tab | Icon | Purpose |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Home** | House | Dashboard — flat pulse, today's tasks, all sections |
| **Duties** | Check circle | Personal task queue, rotation history, swap requests |
| **Log** | + FAB (violet square) | Quick actions: add expense, mark done, request swap |
| **Money** | Card | Expense splits, balances, settlement flow |
| **Nest** | People | Flat identity, members, settings, reliability rankings |

**Active state:** Violet pill background `rgba(124,58,237,0.12)` behind icon + label. Inactive: muted gray, no background.

**Why "Nest" not "Profile":** The last tab is about the flat, not the user. Settings, member management, invite codes, and flat identity all live here. "Nest" ties to the flat name convention (Falcon Nest, etc.) and reinforces the home-first philosophy.

**Why "Duties" not "Tasks":** Implies personal responsibility and rotation — more accurate than the generic word "Tasks."

**Why "Log" not "+":** The FAB is specifically for logging something. "+" is meaningless.

## 14. Product Principles

### Core Principles
1. **Fairness is the product.** Every feature exists to make shared living more fair.
2. **Zero friction onboarding.** Flat live in under 2 minutes.
3. **Transparency by default.** All members see everything — visibility is accountability.
4. **The system is the authority.** Nobody should have to nag.
5. **Mobile first, always.** Most users open the app standing in the kitchen.
6. **Real-time or nothing.** If changes aren't instant, trust breaks down.

### UI Design Principles (Established This Session)
7. **Cards are contexts, not boxes.** Each section has one purpose. Bills ≠ Money ≠ Tasks ≠ Activity. Never mix contexts within a card.
8. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** The task name is the hero. No hero images, no decorative illustrations — the content itself is the design.
9. **No decorative UI.** Every visual element must carry information or enable action.
10. **Separate what is different.** Recurring bills and one-off expense splits are different financial objects. Always display them separately.
11. **Conditional visibility.** Sections that aren't relevant right now should not appear.
12. **Warm, not cold.** All surface and text colour choices carry a warm undertone — not the cold blue-black of a fintech dashboard.
13. **The home screen is the product.** If the home screen is clear, fast, and actionable, every other screen inherits that trust.
